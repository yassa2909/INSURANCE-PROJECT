


# 🧪 1. Basic Understanding Questions (Easy)

These test **simple retrieval**

* What is the main purpose of the Tata AIG MediCare Policy?
* What does the policy cover in general?
* What is meant by hospitalization?
* What is an accident according to the policy?
* What is a pre-existing disease?

# 📚 2. Definition-Based Questions

These test **precise extraction**

* Define “Day Care Treatment”
* What is meant by “Medical Expenses”?
* What is “Cashless facility”?
* What is “Grace Period”?
* What is “Domiciliary Hospitalization”?

---

# 🏥 3. Benefits-Related Questions (Important)

These test **multi-chunk retrieval**

* What benefits are covered under the policy?
* What is covered under in-patient treatment?
* What are pre and post hospitalization expenses?
* What is the ambulance cover limit?
* What is the AYUSH benefit?

---

# 📄 4. List-Type Questions (VERY IMPORTANT for RAG)

These test **completeness + multi-chunk synthesis**

* What documents are required to make a claim?
* List all benefits provided under the policy
* What are the exclusions in the policy?
* What are the conditions for bariatric surgery coverage?
* What are the criteria for a hospital under this policy?

👉 These are the **hardest and most important** for your system

---

# ⚠️ 5. Exclusions Questions (Tricky)

These test **negative filtering + accuracy**

* What is not covered under this policy?
* Are cosmetic surgeries covered?
* Is infertility treatment covered?
* Are experimental treatments covered?
* Are adventure sports injuries covered?

---

# ⏳ 6. Waiting Period Questions

These test **numerical + policy logic understanding**

* What is the waiting period for pre-existing diseases?
* What is the 30-day waiting period rule?
* What is the waiting period for specific diseases?

---

# 🔁 7. Policy Rules & Conditions

These test **deeper understanding**

* What is the renewal policy?
* What is the grace period for renewal?
* What happens in case of fraud?
* What is portability in health insurance?
* What is migration in this policy?

---

# 💡 8. Scenario-Based Questions (Advanced 🔥)

These test **reasoning + context combination**

* If a person is hospitalized for 3 days, what benefits apply?
* Can a claim be made for treatment taken at home?
* What happens if multiple policies are held?
* Can treatment outside India be covered?
* What happens if a claim is delayed?

---

# 🎯 9. Evaluation Questions (Best for testing your RAG quality)

Use these to check **accuracy % like we did earlier**

* What documents are required to make a claim?
* Explain all hospitalization-related benefits
* List all exclusions related to medical treatments
* What are all the benefits under Section 2?
* What conditions must be met for claims approval?
